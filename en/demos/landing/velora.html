Protocol

How we work with you.

Four stages. None of them is skipped, and the first one is free.

Start with the consultation
  1. 01

    Consultation

    We examine your skin and listen to what bothers you and what you are hoping for. If the treatment you came in for is not the right one, we say so here.

    45 minutes · no charge

  2. 02

    Personalised plan

    You get the whole protocol in writing: sessions, timings, total cost, and what result it is reasonable to expect.

    Delivered the same day

  3. 03

    Treatment

    Sessions at the clinic with the same practitioner from start to finish. No surprises in the price or in the procedure.

    As per protocol

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Follow-up at two and at eight weeks, with comparison photography and adjustments if the result calls for them.

    Included in the plan
