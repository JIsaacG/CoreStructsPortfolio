AUREA in pictures.

32 scenes of the campus, the classrooms, the court, the stage and the lab. All of them drawn: AUREA does not exist and there is nothing to photograph.

Every scene is drawn with the same graphic vocabulary — ground, grid, mass, line, figure and a single accent — because a gallery only works as a gallery if its pieces read as the same hand. A stock photograph of real people would be the one dishonest element on a site whose entire argument is that everything in it is declared fiction.

What this site is
Demonstration · fictional institution

Project by CoreStruct

This experience is a demonstration of how we design and build the digital ecosystem of an educational institution.

I want a portal like this →

A portfolio concept. AUREA is a fictional institution. This site represents no real school, institute or university. The programmes, dates, costs, scholarships, figures and people are inventions created solely to demonstrate what the platform can do. None of it should be read as academic information, as an offer of a place, or as any institution's eligibility criteria.
