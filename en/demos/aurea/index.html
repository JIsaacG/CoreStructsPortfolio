042027 admissions

Your route into AUREA starts here.

Six steps, between September and March. The upper secondary process and the higher education one share a shape and almost nothing else, so they are kept separate.

Start my applicationSee the requirements
  1. 01

    Explore the programmes

    Six degrees. Each one publishes its study plan, graduate profile, costs and where it leads before you have to ask.

  2. 02

    Complete the application

    Online. Digital Design also asks for six portfolio pieces; Psychology, a one-page personal statement.

  3. 03

    Submit your documents

    Your certificate or proof of completion, a three-year transcript and ID. The registry validates them within five working days.

  4. 04

    Entrance test and interview

    Reasoning and reading comprehension, weighted as each degree requires. The interview is with the programme lead.

  5. 05

    Admission decision

    An offer letter with a suggested first-term timetable, and the scholarship decision if you applied for one.

  6. 06

    Enrolment

    Choosing modules with your academic adviser, paying the enrolment fee, and activating your institutional email and the virtual campus.

Key dates

  • 15SEP

    Admissions open

    The online application opens for upper secondary and higher education.

  • 30NOV

    First priority deadline

    Applications received by this date are decided first and compete for the full scholarship fund.

  • 15ENE

    Scholarship applications close

    The last day to submit the means-test file and the supporting letters.

  • 10FEB

    Entrance test

    A single sitting, on campus, at the Central Campus. A second sitting on 24 February.
