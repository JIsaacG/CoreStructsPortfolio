A portfolio concept. AUREA is a fictional institution. This site represents no real school, institute or university. The programmes, dates, costs, scholarships, figures and people are inventions created solely to demonstrate what the platform can do. None of it should be read as academic information, as an offer of a place, or as any institution's eligibility criteria.
