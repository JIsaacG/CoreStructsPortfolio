Admissions

15 September 2026 for the 2027 school year, at both levels. The first priority deadline closes on 30 November, and it is the one that competes for the full scholarship fund.

Yes. The application takes a first choice and a second. If there is no place on the first, the file moves to the second automatically, with no documents to submit again.

The application is still accepted as long as places remain. What changes is the order decisions are made in, and how much of the scholarship fund is left — it goes first to the priority applications.

There are two sittings per round: 10 and 24 February. You may take both, and the better result counts.

Payments and costs

It depends on the level and the programme. Upper secondary is charged monthly and higher education by subject. The fee calculator gives a full estimate for a term, additional charges included.

7 % off the term total if you pay before classes start. It stacks with a scholarship and with the sibling discount, up to a ceiling of 80 % of the fee.

Student Finance will reschedule the term's balance without a surcharge once per school year, where something unforeseen has happened. It is worth coming in before the due date rather than after.

10 % off the monthly fee for a second child and 15 % for a third, while both are enrolled at the same time.

Yes. Tenth grade is common to all three baccalaureates, so the change is settled with a request to the Registry before enrolment closes for the second year.

Between twenty-four and thirty-two. No upper secondary class goes above thirty-two students.

Yes, it is a graduation requirement on both technical baccalaureates. The programme office arranges the place and the agreement with the employer.

University

Five is a full load. You may take between three and six; more than six needs the programme lead's authorisation and an average above 85.

Yes. Four of the six degrees run evening classes and three offer an online or blended mode. Around 40 % of third- and fourth-year students work.

No. The award does not distinguish the mode. What changes is the on-site lab requirement: the online mode attends four Saturdays a term on the programmes that have labs.

The Academic Registry assesses credit transfer against each subject's syllabus. The decision is issued within ten working days and can be requested before you enrol.

Scholarships

Two scholarships do not stack, but a scholarship does stack with the sibling or employer discounts, up to a ceiling of 80 % of the fee.

The scholarship goes under review for one cycle. If the average recovers, it continues; if not, it is reduced or suspended — with Student Wellbeing alongside you either way.

No. The simulator on this site is a demonstration: it indicates which schemes a profile could apply for, and it does not assess, does not reserve and does not commit anything. The decision is issued by Student Wellbeing after reviewing the complete file.

International students

Yes. The Academic Registry verifies your prior study and issues the credit-transfer decision within ten working days; the letter of acceptance starts the visa process, which the International Office helps with.

It helps with the application and issues the supporting documents. The decision rests with the immigration authorities and not with the institution.

The institution has no halls of its own. The International Office keeps an indicative list of vetted accommodation near the campus and assigns a tutor for the first term.

Documents

From the student portal, or with the Academic Registry form. Certificates take three working days and full transcripts ten.

It depends on the level. Upper secondary asks for the ninth-grade certificate, a birth certificate and the guardian's ID; higher education asks for the school-leaving award or a statement of completion, plus the transcript for all three years.
