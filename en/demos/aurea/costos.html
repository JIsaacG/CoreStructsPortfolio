03Ways to pay

How you pay.

Monthly plan, no surcharge

Ten equal instalments for upper secondary, five per term for higher education. It is the default plan and costs no more than paying in parts.

Early payment discount

7 % off the term's total if it is settled before classes start.

Sibling discount

10 % off the second child's instalment and 15 % off the third, combinable with a scholarship up to a cap of 80 %.

Employer agreement

Partner employers with a current agreement get 12 % for their staff and immediate family.

Rescheduling payments

If something unexpected happens, Student Finance will reschedule the term's balance with no surcharge, once per school year.
