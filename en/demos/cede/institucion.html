The institution

Coordinate, to move forward.

We coordinate institutional capabilities to turn information, dialogue and planning into better decisions about education.

Founded
2021 (fictional)
Legal status
A coordinating and public information body
Remit
National

01Mandate

What the Council does, and what it does not.

Coordinating is not administering: that difference defines everything else.

The Council does not run schools and does not stand in for the institutions that make up the system. Its role is different and complementary: to maintain a shared reading of the state of education, to order the conversation between those who decide, and to put on public record what was agreed.

It does that with three instruments. The first is information: a single statistical base, documented and open, that lets people argue about facts rather than impressions. The second is planning: a long-term national plan with verifiable targets and a public progress dashboard. The third is participation: formal forums where the public, teachers and employers have their say before a policy is approved, not after.

02Mission and vision

What the institution is working towards.

Mission

To produce and coordinate the information, the planning and the forums the education system needs in order to decide on the evidence.

Vision

An education system that knows its own state in real time, plans ten years ahead, and answers to the public for every target.

03Functions

Six functions, one purpose.

Each function corresponds to a concrete instrument the portal makes public.

01

Education information

Consolidate, validate and publish the national education system's statistics, with a documented methodology and open data by default.

02

National planning

Draw up the long-term education plan, translate it into measurable targets, and track its delivery in public.

03

Policy monitoring

Assess how policies in force are being applied, and issue technical recommendations to the institutions responsible.

04

Inter-institutional coordination

Bring the system's bodies together — public education, higher education, vocational training and local government — around a common agenda.

05

Public participation

Organise public consultations, forums and technical round tables, and publish how each contribution fed into the final decision.

06

Research and foresight

Produce studies on the system's medium-term challenges, and anticipate the skills the country will need.

04Principles

The rules the Council imposes on itself.

01

Evidence before opinion

No recommendation leaves the Council without the statistical base that supports it.

02

Public by default

What the Council produces is public, unless an express rule holds it back.

03

Institutional neutrality

The Council informs and coordinates; it does not promote government programmes or party positions.

04

Territorial equity

Every national figure is published disaggregated: an average that hides a gap tells you nothing.

05

Continuity

The statistical series and the plans carry across administrations.

06

Accessibility

Public information is designed to be used by anyone, without exception.

06A joined-up system

The education system as a network.

No single institution transforms education on its own. The Council sits at the centre of the diagram because it convenes, not because it commands: every node keeps its own powers and brings a capability the others do not have.

Public education

Running the school system, and school-level data.

Higher education

Teacher training and education research.

Vocational training

Technical provision and skills certification.

Universities

Independent evidence and external evaluation.

Local government

Infrastructure, transport and territorial context.

Teachers

Classroom practice and training needs.

Employers

Skills demand and getting people into work.

Civil society

Public scrutiny and the voice of communities.

International cooperation

Technical funding and regional comparison.

07Institutional history

Five years in the building.

  1. 2021

    The Council is created

    Constituted as a coordinating body between the education system's institutions, with a standing technical secretariat.

  2. 2022

    First indicator system

    The minimum set of national education indicators is defined, and the method for calculating them agreed.

  3. 2023

    Opening the data

    The first open education data catalogue is published, with historical series and a variable dictionary.

  4. 2024

    Public consultations

    The prior consultation mechanism is formalised for every education policy of national scope.

  5. 2026

    National Plan 2026–2035

    The long-term education transformation plan is approved, along with its public monitoring dashboard.
