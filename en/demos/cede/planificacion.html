NETP 2026–2035 · Planning

A road map for transforming education.

Ten years, five strands and a commitment you can check: every objective in the plan has a target, an indicator and a date, and its progress is published here as it happens.

01The plan

A plan is a promise with a date on it.

Five strands, fifteen strategic indicators and forty-six verifiable milestones.

The plan organises the coming decade's work on education around five strands. It does not take over the powers of the institutions in the system: it sets out what is to be achieved, how it will be measured, and who answers for each target.

Its cycle is annual. Each year the Council publishes the state of the fifteen strategic indicators, reviews the programmes attached to them, and puts any adjustments out to public consultation. A plan that is never revised stops being a plan and becomes a document.

04The five strands

Each strand, with its objectives and its targets.

Strand 01Quality and learning

That every student learns what the level promises.

41.2 % complete

A system can enrol everyone and still not teach. This strand puts evidence of learning at the centre: periodic national assessment, materials aligned to the curriculum, and targeted support for the schools with the lowest results.

That where a child is born stops deciding how far they go in education.

Strand 03Teacher professionalisation

That teaching is a career with training, support and a path through it.

46.8 % complete

No education reform outruns the quality of its teachers. This strand connects initial training to practice, establishes a continuing training cycle with formal recognition, and organises the career around performance and around staying at hard-to-reach schools.

Strand 04Digital transformation

That digital infrastructure reaches the classroom, not just the report.

33.1 % complete

Connecting schools is the visible part; keeping the connection up, training the people who use it, and producing content of our own is the part that decides whether it works. This strand moves on all three at once, and publishes working connectivity, not contracted connectivity.
