Education policy

Deciding by method, not by impulse.

An education policy is the framework that orders the system's decisions for years: what is wanted, on what principles, by what means, and how anyone will know whether it worked.

Framework in force
National Education Policy 2026–2035
Prior consultation
Mandatory for every national policy
Monitoring
Half-yearly public report

01What an education policy is

A policy is not a plan, and it is not a programme.

The distinction is not academic: it determines what can be demanded, and for how long.

An education policy is neither a programme nor a government plan. It is the settled agreement that says which public problem is being addressed, what counts as a good outcome, and what instruments will be used to reach it. Plans translate it into targets with dates; programmes deliver it in a particular place.

That distinction has a practical consequence: a policy that changes with every administration never produces measurable effects, because education cycles are longer than political ones. Which is why the Council draws policy up with prior consultation, publishes it in full, and tracks it against indicators agreed in advance.

02Objectives

Five policy objectives.

01

Learning

That every student reaches the learning their level promises, and that it can be demonstrated.

02

Progression

That whoever enters the system goes all the way through it, with no exits that depend on where they live.

03

Equity

That the gap between territories narrows steadily and verifiably.

04

Relevance

That what is taught matches what life and work actually ask for now.

05

Institutional capacity

That the system can measure itself, plan, and account for what it does.

03Principles

How it is made and how it is applied.

Universality

Policy addresses the whole system, not a selected segment of it.

Gradual rollout

Changes are applied in verifiable stages, with resources committed stage by stage.

Evidence

Every line of action declares the indicator it will be judged on.

Participation

Prior consultation is part of drafting, not a formality afterwards.

Transparency

A policy's complete file is public from its first draft.

Continuity

Policy is written for a ten-year horizon and is revised, not replaced.

04Strategic priorities

Six lines of action, each with its indicator.

A line of action that does not say how it will be measured cannot be evaluated.

01

Foundational learning

Periodic assessment of learning, materials aligned to the curriculum, and targeted support for the lowest-performing schools.

Monitoring indicator: Basic education completion rate

02

Full progression

Intervention at the system's leaks, with particular attention to the transition from basic to upper secondary.

Monitoring indicator: Transition rate to upper secondary

03

Territorial equity

Targeting investment on the territories with the lowest coverage, and extending local provision where it currently ends at ninth grade.

Monitoring indicator: Urban–rural coverage gap

04

Teachers

Initial training tied to practice, a recognised continuing training cycle, and working conditions at hard-to-reach schools.

Monitoring indicator: Teachers with accreditation

05

Technical education

Rolling updates to provision alongside employers, and tracking of what graduates go on to do.

Monitoring indicator: Technical graduates entering employment

06

Public information

A single, open, documented statistical base as a required input to every education decision.

Monitoring indicator: Datasets published with their methodology

05Monitoring mechanisms

How anyone will know whether the policy worked.

See the plan monitor

Monitoring a policy has three parts. The first is the set of strategic indicators, agreed before the policy is applied and published with their methodology. The second is the half-yearly progress report, which sets what was achieved against what was planned and explains the gaps. The third is the mid-term evaluation, which can change targets where the evidence justifies it — and which puts on public record why.

06Policy agenda

What is in force, what is being drafted, and what is out for consultation.

Publishing the agenda means a policy can be known before it is decided, not after it is approved.
