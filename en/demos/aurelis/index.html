04 / 12Industries

Experience applied to every industry

The technical capability is the same; what changes is the constraint. These are the six where the group has a permanent operation.

Manufacturing and process

Plants where an hour of downtime is measured in product not made. We work on the existing line: extensions without stopping production, utilities under a contracted availability, and measurement that turns loss into a number somebody can attack.

See capabilities for manufacturing
  • Line extension within a bounded shutdown window
  • Utilities: steam, compressed air, refrigeration and process water
  • Predictive maintenance on rotating equipment
  • Batch traceability and statistical process control
  • Energy efficiency by consumption centre

Application

A three-line food plant: consumption was instrumented per line and the staggered start-up of the compressors was rescheduled. Contracted peak demand came down without touching production.

06 / 12Why Aurelis

Four reasons you can check

These are not brand values. They are the four things a client can verify before signing and audit afterwards.

01

Experience

350 projects delivered since 1998, across manufacturing, energy, infrastructure and logistics. References are given with a name, a scope and a contact, not as a wall of logos.

02

Precision

Every proposal starts from a baseline measured on site. If the data does not exist, it is gathered before we quote; no commitment is made on an inherited estimate.

03

Scalability

The same organisation handles a three-week intervention and a three-year multi-country programme, because engineering, the workshop and operations are in-house capabilities rather than a chain of subcontracts.

04

Support

The team that designs takes part in commissioning. There is no throwing it over the wall: whoever signed the calculation answers for how it behaves in service.

07 / 12Methodology

From strategy to delivery

Four stages, each with a closed deliverable. The client can stop the programme at the end of any of them and be left with something useful on its own.

01

Understand

On-site measurement, a review of the file, and interviews with the people who run the asset.

Deliverable · Diagnostic with a baseline

02

Design

Costed options with their risk and their schedule, and the engineering for the one chosen.

Deliverable · Engineering and a firm budget

03

Implement

Construction, installation or rollout, with a weekly progress cut verified on site.

Deliverable · A tested and documented asset

04

Optimise

Supported operation, indicators at steady state, and one committed improvement per quarter.

Deliverable · Quarterly performance report
