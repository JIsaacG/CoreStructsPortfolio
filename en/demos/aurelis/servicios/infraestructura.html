Infrastructure in one line

We build and install industrial and civil works, accountable for the schedule, the cost and the commissioning of the asset.

  • Industrial civil works: foundations, structures and pavements
  • Mechanical installation of static and rotating equipment
  • Medium- and low-voltage electrical installations
  • Instrumentation and control systems
  • Testing, pre-commissioning and commissioning
  • Permits, safety and site personnel management

01 / 05Benefits

What changes for the client

One accountable party

Civil, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation under one contract: there is no boundary where two contractors point at each other.

Physical progress, not declared progress

The weekly cut is measured against quantities installed and verified on site, not against the percentage the programme expected.

Shutdowns planned to the hour

Intervention windows are planned hour by hour, with the isolation sequence, the tests and the return to service rehearsed before the shutdown.

A handover you can operate from

The asset is handed over with a quality dossier, signed test certificates, as-built drawings and training for the people who will run it.

02 / 05Applications

Where it applies

New-build plant

FAQFrequently asked questions

What clients ask before signing

How is schedule risk handled?
With intermediate contractual milestones and a contingency reserve declared separately. If a milestone is at risk, the report says so that same week, not at handover.
Do you work on lump-sum contracts?
Yes, when the engineering is far enough along for the scope to be closed. With incomplete engineering we prefer unit rates: a lump sum over an open scope ends in a claim.
Who is accountable for subcontractor safety?
Aurelis. There is one safety plan for the site, and subcontracted personnel get the same induction and the same audits.
What happens if an unforeseen condition turns up?
It is recorded, priced and presented before it is carried out. No additional work is invoiced without prior written authorisation.
